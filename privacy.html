What this site collects

If you submit the form, I collect your email address. When you arrive from an ad or a tagged link, I also store the campaign details in the link (such as the Google click ID and UTM parameters) along with the page you landed on, and attach them to your submission.

How it is used

Your email is used to reply to your request. Campaign details are used to understand which marketing sources lead to enquiries. I do not sell your information.

Tools involved

This site uses Google Tag Manager, Google Ads and HubSpot. These services may set cookies or similar identifiers. Campaign details are stored in your browser for up to 90 days.

Your choices

You can clear your browser storage at any time. To ask me to delete your data, reply to any email I send you.
